Project E – Blue Jay LED Light Show

This project involves pushing the bounds of electric lighting and computer systems to create an awesome lightshow/display for the lab.

Teammates

Scott Domboski & Andrew Heyeh

Objectives

  • Create an materials list and order parts to achieve the objectives
  • Assemble the Display
  • Program the Lights to display some great message to inspire future Blue Jays

Outcome

Students demonstrate virtualization, scalability, and system management skills.
